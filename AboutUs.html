Welcome to "Scene it?" —> a fun and interactive quiz all about animation!

About the website

"Scene it?" is an independent project made by Heather Rosero, a year 11 digital student, for her level 1 digital internal.

This website mas made for anyone who loves animation, whether you're already familiar with the principles, or are just starting to learn about it. The goal here is to make learning the basics of animation a little more enjoyable by turning it into something interactive! This quiz features multiple questions based around animation principles, history, and general facts. At the end, you recieve a score showing how many questions you got right. Don't worry if you don't get everything correct though, the quiz is also a chance to learn something new!

Autobiography

Hi! I'm Heather, the person behind Scene it?

When I first started this project, I actually had no idea what to make it for and about, animating was just the first thing that came to mind and was meant to be a placeholder until i could figure out what I really wanted my website to be about. After a while though, I realized that animating IS what i wanted to make my webiste about, it's what I love to do most in the world so making a quiz out of it wouldn't be much of a hard task.

(Spoiler: It was a hard task)

But in the end, I had fun making it, and i hope you (the reader) have fun taking the quiz and learning more about animation. I also hope you enjoy the website, and that it is easy to navigate and understand. Keep in mind though, this website was made just for fun, (but also so I can pass my digital internal, so not everything is perfect), but I hope you enjoy it anyway!

Have fun learning!!
