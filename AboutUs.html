About the website

"Scene it?" is a quiz made for animators, or anyone with animating knowledge, wanting to test how much they know. It's also made for those wanting to learn more about animation, and its principles. The quiz is made up of multiple questions, with a score out of how much you got right at the end of it. "Scene it?" is a website made by Heather Rosero, a year 11 digital student, for her level 1 digital internal.

When I made this website, I actually had no idea what to make it for and about, animating was just the first thing that came to mind and was meant to be a placeholder until i could figure out what I really wanted my website to be about. After a while though, I realized that animating IS what i wanted to make my webiste about, it's what I love to do most in the world so making a quiz out of it wouldn't be much of a hard task.

(Spoiler: It was a hard task)

But in the end, I had fun making it, and i hope you (the reader) have fun taking the quiz and learning more about animation. I also hope you enjoy the website, and that it is easy to navigate and understand. Keep in mind though, this website was made just for fun, (but also so I can pass my digital internal, so not everything is perfect), but I hope you enjoy it anyway!
