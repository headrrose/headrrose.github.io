Help page

To start the quiz

  • Press the "Start Quiz" button in the top left corner to begin!
  • You can also press the "Home" link located to the right of the nav bar, then select the "☆ Start quiz ☆" button underneath the big title.

Having more trouble?

  • My quiz isn't starting. Try reloading the page or pressing "start quiz" again.
  • How do I go back to the homepage? Click the "Home" link on the right side of the navbar.
  • I want to learn more about this website. Click the "About Us" link on the right side of the navbar to find out more about "Scene it?" and why it was created.
  • I don't understand an animation term. Hover over the "Learn" link located near the right of the navbar. A dropbox should appear, you can pick what you'd like to learn about animation. It's meant to help you understand animation terms.
