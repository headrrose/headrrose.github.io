About the website

"Scene it?" is a quiz made for animators, or anyone with animating knowledge, wanting to test how much they know, it's also made for those wanting to learn more about animation, and its principles. The quiz is made up of multiple questions, with a score out of how much you know at the end. This website was made by Heather Rosero, a year 11 digital student, for her level 1 digital internal.
