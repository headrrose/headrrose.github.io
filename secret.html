Team Rocket's Members

The iconic Team Rocket consists of 3 members, together they relentlessly chase Ash and his Pikachu.

  1. Jessie/Musashi

    Jessie is a strong-willed, confident, and somewhat haughty Team Rocket member who views herself as an actress, highly values her long hair as her "lifeline," and balances her narcissistic or selfish traits with a compassionate, care for James and Meowth. Overworld and promotional profiles also highlight her striking villainous appearance and her relentless pursuit of Ash and Pikachu.

  2. James/Kojiro

    A member of Team Rocket. He is kind-hearted, making him easy to deceive. He has a deep love for Pokémon and extensive knowledge about them. He has a hobby of collecting things he gets obsessed with, such as bottle caps, Pokémon cards, and Poké Balls. If he stayed quiet, he would look like a hero-level handsome guy, yet he continues to play the role of the one getting defeated every day. Despite being a villain, his kind nature means he is constantly dominated by his colleague, Musashi/Jessie.

  3. Meowth/Nyarth

    A member of Team Rocket who travels alongside Musashi and Kojirō. He is a truly extraordinary Pokémon who, through immense and painful personal effort in his past, taught himself how to speak human language and walk perfectly upright on two legs. Because he spent all of his biological energy and potential learning these human traits, he lost the ability to use his own species' signature move, 'Pay Day.' He acts as the translator whenever the team encounters other Pokémon and frequently considers himself the true, practical brain of the operations.
